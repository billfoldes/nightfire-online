Smoother online play on fast monitors, and the sniper scope's blur no longer lags behind the view.

- **Smooth online frames** (F1, next to Game frame rate, on by default): online play keeps the game at 60 steps a
  second on every PC so the players stay together, and the screen now shows up to your frame rate cap anyway (set
  Frame rate cap, or Game frame rate, above 60), the frames between two steps blended from them. The picture is up
  to a 60th of a second behind the game; the game itself is not changed. Off: 60 frames a second online, as before.
- **The sniper scope** (the Paris van): the blurred view around the sight is the view of that moment. It showed a
  frame or two earlier's, like a layer over the scene at another angle.

**Get the game ready**. See the [v2.0.0 notes](https://github.com/billfoldes/nightfire-online/releases/tag/v2.0.0)
for everything in NightFire Online.
