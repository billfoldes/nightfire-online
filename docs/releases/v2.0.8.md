Co-op and match fixes, new host settings, and the game running above 60 frames a second.

**Co-op**
- **Extra enemies no longer stand in walls or block halls.** Each extra copy now looks for a clear spot with floor
  under it beside the original, toward the walls, and is left out if there is none. Nightshift's guards now get their
  extra copies too.
- **The mission fails when** (F1, Online, host): the last player down (as before), player 1 down, or anyone down.
- **Pickups** (F1, Online, host): Shared (as before), or Separate, where every player can take each ammo, armor and
  weapon pickup once.
- **A door's keypad is shown and played only by the player who used it**; the others keep playing.
- **Driving and sniper missions:** **My own view on the mounted guns** (on by default) looks along your own aim on
  your screen, and **Show the other players' reticles** (off by default) draws theirs, smaller and fainter, in
  their colours. Both are under F1, Online, You (this PC).

**Matches**
- **No phantom fourth player with 3 people.** In a 3-player match each player's full-screen view no longer has the
  007 panel and the countdown from the split screen's empty quarter over it; the clock is at the top.
- **Bots 7 and 8 are on the team they play for.** The Start page sometimes showed them on the other side in team
  modes such as capture the flag.
- If the game ever freezes, its log now records where it stopped and a dump goes to the CrashDumps folder. Please
  send both.

**Everyone**
- **Game frame rate** (F1, game options): 60, your monitor's refresh, 120, 144, 165 or 240. The game itself runs at
  that rate, on foot and driving, at the right speed. Online play stays at 60.
- **Unlock every reward**: everything the cheat codes give, without touching the save.
- **Master volume** (F1, General), 50% by default, and a **stick dead zone** (F1, Controls) for a stick that drifts.
- **F1 Controls** lists what each key does, on foot and driving.
- The **− and +** of a number setting's box no longer close it.
- **The launcher remembers your disc** when you install into a new folder.

Every player needs this same release. Already have the launcher? Open **NightFireOnlineLauncher.exe** and press
**Update**. New players: download **NightFireOnlineLauncher.exe** below, choose your disc and press
**Get the game ready**. See the [v2.0.0 notes](https://github.com/billfoldes/nightfire-online/releases/tag/v2.0.0)
for everything in NightFire Online.
